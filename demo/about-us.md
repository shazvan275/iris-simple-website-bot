# About Us

This is dummy About Us content for the Iris demo knowledge base.

IIRSI is presented here as a sample event experience for testing website chatbot answers.

The event brings together attendees, speakers, sponsors, and support teams in one shared venue.

The organizing team focuses on registration support, session coordination, venue guidance, and attendee assistance.

Guests can use Iris to ask general questions about the event, available support, and where to find help during the program.
