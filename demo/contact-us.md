# Contact Us

This is dummy Contact Us content for the Iris demo knowledge base.

For event assistance, guests can visit the support desk near the registration counter.

The support desk can help with badge questions, venue directions, accessibility needs, lost items, and presentation-room issues.

For urgent on-site questions, attendees should contact an event team member or ask the welcome desk for the fastest route to support.

General event questions can also be sent to the sample email address `info@example.com`.
